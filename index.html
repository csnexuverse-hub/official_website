Our Story

Built by academics, for academics

CS Development & Solutions was born from a simple frustration — students with brilliant minds getting stuck not because they lacked intelligence, but because they lacked the right guidance at the right time. We bridge that gap with structured, human-first mentorship that actually builds mastery.

Domain-specific mentors — every student is matched with someone who specialises in their exact field, not a generalist.
Outcome-first methodology — we measure success by your confidence and results, not by session count.
Flexible scheduling — sessions that fit around your university timetable, not the other way around.
Full transparency — you always know where you stand, what you've learned, and what comes next.
